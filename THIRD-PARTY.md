## Archive of Our Own (OTW)

The Archive of Our Own name and logo are trademarks of the Organization for
Transformative Works (OTW). KO3 is unofficial and is not affiliated with or
endorsed by the OTW. The marks are used only to identify the service this
plugin reads.

- `ao3.koplugin/assets/ao3-logo.png` is copied byte-for-byte from the OTW's
  [`otwarchive`](https://github.com/otwcode/otwarchive) repository
  (`public/images/ao3_logos/logo.png`), and `ao3.koplugin/assets/ao3-logo.svg`
  is a vector trace of that PNG. `otwarchive` is licensed
  **GPL-2.0-or-later** by the OTW. These files are used under those terms
  inside this AGPL-3.0 project: GPL-2.0-or-later permits use under GPLv3, and
  AGPLv3 permits combining with GPLv3. The original source is linked above.
- `ao3.koplugin/assets/icons/category-*.svg` and `complete-no.svg` redraw AO3's
  required-tag symbols from its public symbol set, in monochrome for e-ink.
  To the extent they are derivative works of `otwarchive`, they are used under
  the same GPL-2.0-or-later terms.
- `ao3.koplugin/data/fandoms/` is a snapshot of AO3's public fandom listing
  (`/media` and `/media/<category>/fandoms`), generated by
  `tools/build_fandoms.py`. It holds fandom names and work counts only — no
  works, users, comments or other user content. Fandom names remain associated
  with their creators and the OTW; see
  [`ao3.koplugin/data/README.md`](ao3.koplugin/data/README.md).
- Test fixtures under `ao3.koplugin/spec/fixtures/` are short excerpts of AO3
  pages used only by the offline test suite. They are excluded from the
  release archive.

No OTW program code is copied into KO3: every parser is written against the
HTML or JSON that AO3 serves.
